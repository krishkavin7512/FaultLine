Ensemble learning on Nepal's 2015 earthquake

Which house falls next? The last earthquake left the answer key.

After the 2015 Gorkha earthquake, survey teams visited every house in the 11 worst-hit districts outside Kathmandu and graded its damage. FaultLine learns from that survey which houses fall, then predicts three whole districts it has never seen, including the one that held the epicentre.
